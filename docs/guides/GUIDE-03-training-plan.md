# GUIDE-03 — Training Plan

**Status:** Baseline · **Owner:** BA/PM · **When:** Week 8 (go-live) · **Basis:** §2.3 deliverable

The contracted training session for ULink's operations team.

## Audiences & sessions
| Session | Audience | Duration | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Content & SEO | Editors | 60–90 min | GUIDE-01 content, media, translations, publish, SEO fields |
| 2. RFQ, Orders & Debt | Sales | 60 min | RFQ triage, customers, orders, invoices/công nợ, deliveries |
| 3. Admin & Ops | Admin/IT | 45 min | Users/roles, backups overview, OPS-01/02/03 essentials |
| 4. Customer experience | All | 30 min | GUIDE-02 buyer/portal walkthrough |

## Format
Live (in-person or remote) + screen-share on the **staging/production** system using
real workflows, then hands-on practice. **Recorded** for later reference.

## Materials
- GUIDE-01 (CMS Admin), GUIDE-02 (Portal), OPS-02 (Handover).
- Quick-reference cheat sheets (publish, translations, RFQ status flow).
- Recording + slides.

## Hands-on exercises
- Create + publish a product (with VI/EN/JP + SEO).
- Upload a TDS/MSDS and link it.
- Triage an incoming RFQ; create an order + invoice; schedule a delivery.
- Add a customer + user; verify they see only their data.

## Success criteria
Each role can independently perform its core tasks unaided by the end of the session.
Open questions logged and answered; recording + guides handed over.

## Follow-up
Two weeks of post-training Q&A support (part of bug-fixing/handover); recurring needs
roll into the annual maintenance.
