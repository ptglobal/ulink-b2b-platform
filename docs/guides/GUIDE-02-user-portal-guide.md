# GUIDE-02 — End-User / B2B Portal Guide

**Status:** Baseline (screenshots added at handover) · **Audience:** ULink customers (buyers)

How to find products, request quotes, and use the B2B portal on `https://www.ulink…`.

## Language
Pick **VI / EN / JP** in the top-right switcher. Your choice stays as you browse.

## Find products
- Browse **Solutions** (Cleanroom, Packaging) or filter by **Industry** (Electronics,
  Pharmaceutical, Cosmetics, Food & Beverage).
- Use **SKU search** if you know the code.
- On a product page: read **technical specifications**, **download TDS/MSDS**,
  **request a sample**, or **add to your RFQ cart**.

## Request a quote (RFQ)
1. Add products/SKUs to the **RFQ cart** (or use **Quick Order** to add by SKU /
   upload a list).
2. Open the cart, set quantities.
3. Submit with your company + contact details (+ optional message).
4. You'll get a confirmation; ULink Sales follows up with a quote.

## B2B Portal (for registered customers)
Log in (top-right) to:
| Feature | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Summary of orders, outstanding balance, upcoming deliveries |
| **Order History** | Your past and current orders |
| **Scheduled Delivery** | Upcoming delivery dates |
| **Debt / Công nợ** | Your invoices, balances, due dates |
| **Re-order** | Repeat a previous order into a new RFQ cart |

You only ever see **your own** company's data.

## Register / get access
Use **Register** or contact ULink to have an account created and linked to your company.

## Help
Use the **Contact** page for support or sales enquiries.
